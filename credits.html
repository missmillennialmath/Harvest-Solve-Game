Harvest & Solve is a math teacher’s independent educational game project.Yes it was made with AI; yes I am mildly sorry. No, I cannot afford a whole coding team or to create a sidebusiness with a math teacher's salary, and yes I love my job.

This is a labor of love to try and fill a gap in the free games market for older high school students with a free open source project. I cannot afford to do this project with human labor purely, but I do beleive there is a place for it in this world.

Thank your for your grace and understanding. I hope this project serves you.

Development: Dianna "DJ" Johnson, Miss Millennial Math, in partnership with her chatGPT premium account, basic 100-300 level coding classes from ASU, and some gumption. Menu scenery: temporary original pixel-style vector artwork.
