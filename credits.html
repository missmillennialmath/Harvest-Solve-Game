Credits

Harvest & Solve is a math teacher’s independent educational game project. Yes, it was made with AI; yes, I am mildly sorry. No, I cannot afford a whole coding team or to create a side business on a math teacher's salary, and yes, I love my job.

I want to see a project like this through, but I also don't want to leave the classroom to make it happen. This was my solution: imperfect, but hopefully lasting and worthwhile in its use and reuse.

This is a labor of love to try to fill a gap in the free games market for older high school students with a free, open-source project. I cannot afford to complete this project using human labor alone, but I do believe there is a place for it in this world.

Thank you for your grace and understanding. I hope this project serves you.

Development: Dianna "DJ" Johnson, Miss Millennial Math, in partnership with her ChatGPT premium account, 100–300 level coding classes from ASU, and some gumption.

Menu scenery: AI-generated pixel-art marketplace artwork, developed through collaborative design and iterative refinement, with custom CSS animation layers.
