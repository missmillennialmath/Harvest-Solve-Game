How to Play

Harvest & Solve is a farming RPG for Algebra I. In the planned game, solve math problems to care for crops and animals, earn gold and improve your farm. Use hints and retry problems as needed.

Saving your progress

Protect your progress!

Browser autosaves may not work reliably on school-managed Chromebooks or devices with parental controls, monitoring or filtering software like Securly. Always export your save before leaving. Keep the file somewhere safe, such as school-approved cloud storage.

Open Continue / Save Menu and select Export Save. To restore progress, select Import Save from the main menu and choose your JSON file. Exported filenames use charactername_mm_dd__yy.json (for example, unnamed_farmer_11_25__26.json). You can keep as many external backups as you want. The game keeps only one active browser save. No real name is required.

Remember: your chickens are counting on you. Back up responsibly.
