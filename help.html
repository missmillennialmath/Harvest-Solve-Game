THE FARMER'S GUIDE

How to Play

1. Grow your farm

Harvest & Solve is a farming RPG for Algebra I. In the planned game, you will:

  • Solve math problems to care for crops and animals.
  • Earn gold and improve your farm.
  • Use hints and retry problems as needed.

Coming later: farming and math activities. This build tests saving only.

2. Save your progress

The game keeps one active browser save. No real name is required.

Protect your progress!

Browser autosaves may not work reliably on:

  • School-managed Chromebooks.
  • Devices with parental controls.
  • Devices with monitoring or filtering software like Securly.

Always export your save before leaving the game.

Keep the file somewhere safe, such as school-approved cloud storage.

How to export your save

  1. Open Continue / Save Menu.
  2. Click Export Save.
  3. Find the downloaded JSON file.
  4. Keep it somewhere safe.

How to import your save

  1. Open Harvest & Solve.
  2. Click Import Save on the main menu.
  3. Choose your previously exported JSON file.
  4. If asked, confirm replacement of the active adventure.
  5. Click Continue to resume your adventure.

Before replacing an adventure: Export your current save first if you want to keep it.

3. Find and organize your saves

Exported save files follow this naming pattern:

charactername_mm_dd__yy.json

Example:

unnamed_farmer_11_25__26.json
  • You may rename your save files.
  • You may keep as many external backups as you want.

Remember: your chickens are counting on you. Back up responsibly.
